# PetApp3‑Kuro — Netherland Dwarf Edition

Kuro is a pedigree Netherland Dwarf rabbit known for his quiet elegance and beautifully expressive eyes.  
His glossy jet‑black coat has a unique layered texture—each hair is white at the base and turns black toward the tip, creating a soft shimmering glow under diffused light.  
He carries a calm, intelligent presence, and every image reflects the warmth and subtle wisdom in his gaze.

This Kuro Edition presents a complete set of AI‑generated images based on original live‑action photos of Kuro.  
All generated assets were carefully crafted to preserve his real appearance, personality, and gentle emotional expression.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Full AI‑generated media set**  
  All files in `generated/*.png` are AI‑generated using real Kuro photos as reference.

- **Dynamic behavior states (n1–p12)**  
  Includes joyful binky jumps, playful movements, calm resting poses, and affectionate reactions.

- **Affection gesture (p12)**  
  When hearing “I love you,” Kuro gently lowers his head and tenderly licks my hand with quiet affection.

- **Name‑call reaction**  
  When you call his name, Kuro performs a joyful binky—just like when responding to the voice command “Good boy.”

- **Smile reaction**  
  When reacting to the camera (smile request), Kuro performs the playful **p1 (play)** state.

- **English voice commands**  
  Kuro reacts to simple English voice commands such as:  
  *sit, sleep, play, come here, good boy, happy, paw, meal, water, toilet, fetch, house, stand, bath, smile, I love you*  
  These commands map directly to PetApp3 states (n1–p12).

- **Consistent worldbuilding**  
  Structured and written in the same style as the Taro Edition and Frosty Edition, keeping a unified PetApp3 series identity.

---

## 🐇 Appearance

Kuro’s appearance is defined by:

- Glossy jet‑black coat with subtle white roots  
- Each hair white at the base and black at the tip  
- Gentle, intelligent eyes with a warm brown tint  
- Upright ears with soft brown inner coloring  
- Short tail with gray undertones  
- Calm and quiet elegance

These details are fully integrated into `pet_profile.json` and reflected across all generated images.
